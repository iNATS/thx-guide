# **App Name**: Timimoun Oasis Guide

## Core Features:

- Road Map Display: Display detailed road maps of Timimoun and surrounding areas using reusable map components.
- Events Calendar: Show a calendar of upcoming cultural events, festivals, and tours in Timimoun using a dynamic calendar component.
- QR Code Scanner: Use the phone's camera to scan QR codes placed around Timimoun. Information is accessed via a QR scanner component. Scan results may incorporate information from external URLs.
- Contact Support: Provide a direct contact link to tour guides or support personnel within Timimoun. Utilize a contact card component with email and phone options.
- Smart Itinerary Tool: AI tool suggests points of interest based on the user's interests (historical sites, natural wonders, cultural experiences).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Red-orange (#E65A1B) to reflect the 'Red Oasis' theme and desert sands (defined as $primary-color).
- Background color: Desaturated beige (#F2E3D5), evoking the color of traditional architecture in Timimoun (defined as $background-color).
- Accent color: Dark brown (#261A0D), inspired by the earth and the deep shadows cast by the desert sun (defined as $accent-color).
- Headline font: 'Playfair' serif for elegance, matching the blend of traditional and contemporary culture (defined as $headline-font); body font: 'PT Sans' sans-serif, pairing well with Playfair, for body text (defined as $body-font).
- Use icons inspired by Berber motifs and desert landscapes, such as stylized dunes, stars, and traditional patterns (defined as $icon-style).
- Card-based layout with rounded corners, presenting information in a concise and visually appealing way (defined as $card-layout).
- Subtle transitions and animations, such as fading effects when loading new information, to enhance the user experience without being distracting (defined as $transition-style).
